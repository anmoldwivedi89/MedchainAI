# 🌍 The Problem

Counterfeit medicines are one of the most dangerous and under-addressed challenges in healthcare.

Millions of people unknowingly consume fake medicines due to the lack of accessible verification systems.

These medicines can:

* Cause treatment failure
* Contain incorrect ingredients
* Lead to severe health complications
* Damage trust in healthcare systems

Current verification methods are often inaccessible to ordinary consumers.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

MedChain AI is an intelligent healthcare verification platform that combines:

✅ OCR

✅ Artificial Intelligence

✅ Fraud Intelligence

✅ Pharmacy Trust Networks

✅ Geospatial Analysis

✅ Blockchain Verification

to help users determine whether a medicine is potentially genuine or suspicious.

# ✨ Key Features

## 🔍 AI Medicine Verification

Upload a medicine image and instantly receive:

* Medicine Identification
* Manufacturer Validation
* Authenticity Score
* Risk Assessment
* Fraud Intelligence

---

## 📸 OCR Extraction

Powered by Google Vision OCR

Extracts:

* Medicine Name
* Manufacturer
* Batch Number
* Expiry Date

---

## 🤖 AI Analysis

Powered by Gemini

Analyzes:

* Medicine Information
* Packaging Consistency
* Fraud Reports
* Risk Signals

Returns:

* Confidence Score
* Authenticity Score
* Risk Classification

---

## 🏥 Trusted Pharmacies

Discover:

* Nearby Pharmacies
* Verified Stores
* Trust Scores
* Directions

---

## 🚨 Fraud Intelligence

Users can:

* Report suspicious medicines
* Upload evidence
* Create community-driven fraud alerts

---

## 🌍 Fraud Heatmaps

Visualize:

* Counterfeit Hotspots
* High-Risk Areas
* Regional Fraud Trends

---

## ⛓️ Blockchain Verification

Store immutable:

* Medicine Records
* Company Verification
* Pharmacy Verification

using Polygon Blockchain.
